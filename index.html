Privacy Policy

App Name: AquaPulse (Water Tracker)

Last updated: April 16, 2026

Overview

AquaPulse is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. No information is transmitted to external servers.

Local Storage

All data (such as your daily water intake goals and history) is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device.

Notifications

The app may send local reminders to help you stay hydrated. These notifications are generated on-device and do not involve any external services or data sharing.

Third-Party Services

AquaPulse does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
